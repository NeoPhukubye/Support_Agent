COMPANY POLICIES - SupportAI

=== REFUND POLICY ===

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee:
All new subscriptions are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of the initial purchase. To request a refund, contact support@supportai.com with your account email and order number. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

Partial Refunds:
For cancellations after 30 days, we do not offer partial refunds. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

Add-on Purchases:
One-time add-on purchases (extra storage, premium features) are non-refundable after 7 days.

=== PRIVACY POLICY (SUMMARY) ===

Data Collection:
We collect account information, usage data, and support interactions to provide and improve our services.

Data Storage:
All data is stored in AWS data centers with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

Data Sharing:
We never sell your data. We only share with third parties necessary to deliver our service (payment processors, cloud infrastructure).

Data Retention:
Active account data: retained while account is active.
Deleted account data: permanently removed within 30 days.
Backup data: purged within 90 days.

GDPR/CCPA Compliance:
We are fully GDPR and CCPA compliant. You can request data export or deletion at any time through Settings > Privacy.

=== SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) ===

Uptime Guarantee:
- Enterprise plan: 99.99% uptime SLA
- Pro plan: 99.9% uptime SLA
- Free plan: No SLA (best effort)

Support Response Times:
- Critical (service down): 1 hour response, Enterprise only
- High (major feature broken): 4 hours, Pro and Enterprise
- Medium (minor issue): 24 hours, all paid plans
- Low (questions): 48 hours, all paid plans

Scheduled Maintenance:
Maintenance windows are Sundays 2:00 AM - 4:00 AM UTC. We notify customers 48 hours in advance via email and status page.

=== ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY ===

Prohibited activities:
- Using the service for illegal purposes
- Sending spam or unsolicited communications
- Attempting to hack, scrape, or reverse-engineer the platform
- Sharing account credentials with unauthorized users
- Uploading malicious files or malware

Violations:
First offense: Warning and content removal.
Second offense: Temporary account suspension (7 days).
Third offense: Permanent account termination without refund.
